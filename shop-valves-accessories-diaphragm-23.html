Diaphragm Valves

Harrington offers name brand diaphragm valves, providing a broad selection of brands, styles, connection types, materials, and specifications. These valves are designed to control fluid flow by adjusting the entry and exit areas of the media. Available materials include polypropylene and PVDF.
