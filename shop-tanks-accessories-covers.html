Lids/Covers

Tank lids and covers are perfect for preventing rainwater, debris, dust, leaves, and other items from entering wastewater basins. We provide lids and covers made from materials like XLPE, HDLPE, polyethylene, and polypropylene. Our lids can fit a wide range of existing tanks.
