Harrington named 2025 Supplier of the Year by Munters

Recognition from a long-standing data center cooling customer for delivery performance and technical support.

We're proud to announce that Harrington Process Solutions has been named 2025 Supplier of the Year by Munters Data Center Technologies. This recognition reflects the trust we've earned, the strength of our partnership, and the dedication our teams demonstrate every day in serving our customers.

Selected through Munters' annual supplier evaluation process, the award recognizes suppliers that consistently deliver excellence in service, responsiveness, reliability, quality, and partnership. Being named Supplier of the Year is a testament to the value our team brings to one of our most important strategic customers.

About Munters

Munters is a global leader in climate control solutions, delivering systems that precisely manage humidity, temperature, and air quality in some of the world's most demanding environments. Their technologies support critical industries including data centers, food production, pharmaceutical manufacturing, and battery production.

Munters Data Center Technologies focuses on cooling solutions for the rapidly growing data center market, one of the fastest-growing and most mission-critical industries in the world today. Over the past three years, Munters has become one of Harrington's most important strategic national accounts, making this recognition especially meaningful.

From one opportunity to trusted partnership

This partnership began with a single opportunity at Munters' Selma, Texas facility. From there, our teams demonstrated what true partnership looks like: delivering mission-critical products, navigating supply chain challenges, providing technical expertise, and maintaining the responsiveness and reliability customers depend on.

That commitment helped expand the relationship across multiple facilities and grew the account approximately ninefold in three years. More importantly, it built the trust that serves as the foundation of our partnership today.

Awards like this aren't earned through a single project or transaction. They're earned through hundreds of daily actions that make a difference: the quote returned quickly, the order delivered on time, the problem solved before it impacts the customer, and the teamwork that happens behind the scenes every day.

From national accounts and sales to supply chain, specialty teams, category management, operations, customer service, finance, and our branch teams across the country, this award is a direct reflection of their hard work, expertise, and commitment.

Harrington is very deserving of the Supplier of the Year Award. I believe in partnerships and what Bill Sovik and the entire Harrington team has done to help build this partnership is second to none. Munters DCT looks forward to continued growth and success with Harrington.

Mark Journell, Global Director of Supply Chain, Munters

Looking ahead

As Munters continues to expand its presence in the data center market, we have an opportunity to further strengthen our partnership and deliver even greater value. This award reinforces what is possible when we work together, stay focused on customer success, and consistently deliver on our commitments.

We are grateful to our partners at Munters for their trust and collaboration, and we look forward to continuing our shared success.
