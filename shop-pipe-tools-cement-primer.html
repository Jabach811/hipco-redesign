Cement and Primers

Harrington is your reliable supplier of high-quality cement, adhesives, and primers, offering a wide array of brands based on application type. Primers are essential for cleaning PVC, softening its outer layer to remove grime and release surface molecules, while cement softens PVC to facilitate bonding between pieces. We also carry two part adhesives. Our material selection includes Metal, PVC, PTFE, CPVC, acrylic adhesive, ABS, PVC/CPVC, and primer.
