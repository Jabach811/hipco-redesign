Butterfly Valves

At Harrington, we provide premium butterfly valves, available in a wide variety of brands, styles, connection types, materials, and specifications. These valves are designed to shut-off flow in large pipelines / piping systems. Our selection includes materials like stainless steel, PVC, PVDF, polypropylene, and PDCPD.
