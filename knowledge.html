Questions

Asked often enough to write down.

Short answers. For anything about your specific line, ask a specialist.

Why is the website called hipco.com when the company is Harrington?
HIPCO stands for Harrington Industrial Plastics Company. The company became Harrington Process Solutions to cover the wider range of flow control work it does now, beyond distributing plastic pipe.
What does Harrington actually do?
We stock and supply the parts that move corrosive, high-purity, and harsh fluids: pipe, fittings, valves, pumps, filtration, instrumentation, tanks, and engineered assemblies. And we help you pick the right ones.
How long has Harrington been in business?
Since 1959. Hank Harrington opened the first counter in Lynwood, California.
How is Harrington different from other industrial distributors?
The person who quotes your job is an application specialist, not a catalog clerk. Ninety branches keep stock local, and one phone number reaches a live person around the clock.
Can I buy online?
Yes. The store at store.hipco.com shows stock, pricing for account holders, and order history, and takes purchase orders or cards.
Do you provide engineering and technical support?
Yes. Material selection, system design review, equipment sizing, troubleshooting, and on-site support through the branch network and the specialty group.
Do you build custom or engineered systems?
Yes. Fabricated spools, skids, valve automation, lined pipe, tanks, and control panels, through Harrington branches and the family companies.
Do you offer 24/7 support?
Yes. Call 1-800-877-4472 any hour and a person answers. Emergency stock pulls and pump service are available for critical applications.
