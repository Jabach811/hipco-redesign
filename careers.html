Why work here

Think big. Act small. Succeed together.

That is the company principle, and it is a fair description of the job. A national company where the branch down the street still makes its own calls.

  1. You learn the tradeNobody arrives knowing the difference between CPVC and PVDF. Harrington University teaches it from day one, and the people at the next desk have been answering those questions for twenty years.
  2. The branch runs itselfManagers decide what to stock, who to hire, and how to handle the customer at the counter. Decisions happen where the work is.
  3. Safety is not negotiableForklifts, drums of acid, and delivery routes are part of the job. So is going home in one piece. Anyone can stop a job that looks wrong.
  4. Room to moveCounter to inside sales, driver to warehouse lead, branch to region. Most of the people running things started somewhere else in the company.
  5. The work is realThe pipe you quote ends up in a water plant, a fab, or a brewery. When it is right, something works that a lot of people depend on.

Life at Harrington

Small teams, long tenures.

A typical branch is a dozen people who know each other's customers by name. Wins get celebrated in the shop, mistakes get talked through instead of buried, and the good ideas usually come from whoever is closest to the problem.

It starts with people: investing in their growth, supporting their development, and creating an environment where they can thrive. That's what drives strong teams, meaningful careers, and real impact for our customers.

Deanna Torrez, Director of Talent Acquisition
