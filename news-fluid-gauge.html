Harrington acquires Fluid Gauge Company

Gauges, instrumentation, and flow control from a company founded in 1932 join the family.

We are excited to welcome Fluid Gauge Company, a family-owned distributor of specialty industrial, sanitary, and process piping and engineered products with over 90 years in business, to the Harrington family of companies. Fluid Gauge operates from two locations in California and serves customers in the food and beverage, biopharma, industrial, municipal, and institutional end markets. Also included in the transaction is A-1 Milmac, a custom hose fabricator wholly owned by FGC.

Fluid Gauge has a strong reputation for technical expertise and quality products and services and has built deep customer and supplier relationships. The company's specialty engineered metal PVF and instrumentation portfolio, along with custom hose fabrication capabilities, complement Harrington's portfolio and broaden the range of solutions Harrington provides to customers.

Fluid Gauge's team is dedicated to delivering top-quality products and excellent customer service, aligning with Harrington's values and creating a strong shared purpose between the teams. We look forward to building an even stronger future as a combined team.

About Fluid Gauge Company

Fluid Gauge Company was established in 1932 as a stocking wholesale distributor of power, marine, and industrial engineering specialties, and continues its legacy of family ownership into the fourth generation. In response to the evolving demands of the industries it serves, the company has expanded into a multifaceted wholesale distributor providing high-quality products for the gas and power sector, pharmaceutical facilities, municipalities, food processing plants, the marine industry, wineries, and more.
