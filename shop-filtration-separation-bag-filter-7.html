Bag Filters

Harrington is a leading supplier of high-quality bag filters. Our extensive selection includes a variety of brands, styles, and specifications. Bag filters are used for the clarification and filtration of fluids with relatively low particle loads. Available materials include polypropylene, polyester, polyester ring, zinc-plated carbon steel, and more.
