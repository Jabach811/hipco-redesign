Environmental commitment

Setting standards. Measuring progress.

From our supplier partners to the people whose lives our products touch, we have a role in environmental stewardship.

Environmental policy statement (PDF)

At Harrington, sustainability means creating long-term value for our stakeholders and society by actively reducing the environmental impact of our products, operations, and value chain. We are committed to continually improving our business activities to pursue high standards of environmental sustainability, particularly in climate protection, emissions reduction, and waste elimination.

Our objectives

  • Conduct operations in compliance with applicable environmental laws and regulations, while setting higher standards for ourselves and our supplier partners.
  • Set clear targets for environmental impact and greenhouse gas reduction for ourselves and our suppliers. Communicate them openly and report publicly on progress to employees, stakeholders, and customers.
  • Assess our environmental impact across the value chain and define strategies to work toward our reduction targets while growing the business.
  • Include environmental risks in our corporate integrated risk assessment and review proposed strategies with the executive leadership team.
  • Implement internationally recognized environmental management systems, audit them independently, and certify our operations where possible.
  • Raise awareness among employees and give them the knowledge, resources, and tools to implement our programs and industry best practices.
  • Engage suppliers to reduce their environmental impact through supply chain initiatives: carbon footprint reduction, water stewardship, waste management plans, and ongoing training.

Climate action

Net zero by 2040.

Near-term actions to decarbonize our operations and value chain, matched with long-term ambition in line with the Paris Agreement.

We have joined the Net Zero movement and are setting explicit goals that build on our existing greenhouse gas (GHG) reduction targets, in support of a more stable climate for future generations.

Our approach

  • Prioritize GHG reductions by addressing inefficiencies and emissions within our value chain wherever possible. We have set a science-based target to reduce GHG by 15% by 2030, in line with global reduction targets. We aim to shift to 100% renewable energy in our facilities and electrify the company fleet wherever feasible, while supporting our manufacturer partners in adopting sustainable production processes.
  • Collaborate with suppliers through collective actions and strategic programs that drive GHG reductions in manufacturing facilities: identifying opportunities across the upstream value chain, setting emission reduction targets through energy efficiency and renewable sourcing, and helping suppliers build climate strategies aligned with our objectives.
  • Pursue a circularity strategy offering product recycling, repair, and resale solutions to keep products at their highest value, reducing the need for virgin materials and minimizing waste. After 2030, we will continue to set science-based GHG reduction targets along our net zero pathway to 2040.
