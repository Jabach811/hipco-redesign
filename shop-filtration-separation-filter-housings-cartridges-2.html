Cartridge Filter Housings

Harrington is dedicated to supplying high-quality cartridge filter housings. Our selection features a wide range of brands, styles, materials, and specifications. Cartridge filter housings are essential for safeguarding water treatment systems and achieving water quality standards. They are extensively used in industrial water filtration, particularly in the food and beverage, pharmaceutical, and reverse osmosis industries. We offer materials such as 304 stainless steel, polypropylene, CPVC, glass-reinforced nylon, and glass-filled polypropylene.
