Pipe Hangers

Harrington is a premier supplier of high-quality pipe hangers and hanging accessories in a range of styles and types. Hangers are essential in construction and plumbing for stabilizing and supporting systems. They help align pipes, reduce vibration, and prevent sagging by suspending them from walls or ceilings. Versatile and reliable, pipe hangers can support various pipe materials, including PVC, copper, and steel. Our accessories include straps, clamps, channels, wall brackets, sleeves and more.
