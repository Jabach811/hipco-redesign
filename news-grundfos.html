Harrington partners with Grundfos in California

Grundfos pumps now stocked and supported through California branches for water and municipal customers.

We are pleased to announce a new partnership between Harrington and Grundfos, aimed at serving customers in the California water utility and municipal markets. Grundfos is a global leader in advanced pump solutions and a pioneer in water technology. The company has been designing, manufacturing, and supplying high-quality pump systems and related technologies since 1945, when it was founded in Denmark.

Through this partnership, Grundfos and Harrington combine their strengths to provide products, services, and technological solutions to customers in California. With Harrington's acquisition of PumpMan, we offer service expertise and technical support that ensure customers receive complete solutions for pump and repair needs.

What customers can expect

  • A strong stocking distributor network. With more than ten strategically located facilities in California, Harrington provides convenient access to Grundfos products, ensuring timely delivery and availability to contractors, repair shops, and municipal customers throughout the state.
  • Digital tools. Harrington's online store and account tools save time on ordering, tracking, and reordering.

Complete pump systems

In addition to a wide range of pumps, Harrington supplies all the components needed to build complete pump systems, customized to the requirements of your industry. Our expertise spans metal plating and finishing, semiconductors, water treatment, wastewater management, biotechnology, and pharmaceuticals.
