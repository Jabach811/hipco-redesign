Water & wastewater

Our oldest market and still our deepest. Chlorine, hypochlorite, alum, caustic, and ferric chloride each eat a different material, so we pick pipe zone by zone, from intake to discharge. Anything that touches drinking water comes NSF-61 certified (the standard that proves a part will not leach into potable water).

  • PVC and CPVC, NSF-61 certified for potable contact
  • PVDF and polypropylene dosing lines for hypochlorite and acids
  • FRP for aeration and large-diameter structural runs
  • True union ball, butterfly, and diaphragm valves
  • pH, ORP, flow, and level instrumentation
  • Metering, centrifugal, and peristaltic pumps

02

Semiconductor & microelectronics

Inside a fab the pipe is part of the yield. Particles, ions, and anything leaching from a pipe wall reach the wafer, so every wetted plastic joint is fused rather than threaded or glued. We supply every fluid system in the building: ultra-pure water, chemical and slurry, bulk gas, process cooling, waste, and cleanroom.

  • PVDF for ultra-pure water and acid loops
  • PFA for the most aggressive bulk chemistries
  • Polypropylene for drains and process waste
  • Electropolished 316L tubing, orbital welded, for gases
  • Double-contained waste with leak detection
  • Fusion equipment, fittings, and tool hook-up assemblies

Standards SEMI F57 · ASTM extractables · Fusion-joined throughout

03

Data centers

Racks are pushing past 100 kW and liquid cooling has become the plant. A coolant leak above a live server row is not a maintenance ticket, it is an outage. We size the chilled water loops, CDU (coolant distribution unit) feeds, and headers, then pre-assemble and test the actuated valve packages before they ship.

  • CPVC Schedule 80 for primary chilled water, glycol to 50%
  • Heat-fused PP-R headers over active server rows
  • PVDF where deionized water or specialty coolants run
  • Double containment with leak detection tied to the BMS
  • Pre-tested actuated valve packages for zone isolation and N+1
  • Glycol compatibility and seal-material review
