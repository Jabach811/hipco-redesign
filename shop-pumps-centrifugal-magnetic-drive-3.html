Magnetic Drive Pumps

Harrington is a supplier of high quality magnetic drive pumps. Magnetic drive pumps, also known as mag-drive pumps, are used in industrial applications to handle hazardous or corrosive liquids. They are often used in industries such as water treatment, the chemical industry, and the photographic industry.
