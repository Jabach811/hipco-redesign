About

Sixty-seven years of picking the right pipe.

Harrington started in 1959 as a repair-parts counter in Lynwood, California. Today it is 90 branches, ten companies, and a person on the phone around the clock. The job has not changed: match the material to the chemistry, and have it on the shelf.

Est. 1959

Built on solving problems.

Hank Harrington saw wholesalers and plants struggling to find plastic parts that would survive what was running through them. He stocked those parts. Within a year the company was distributing full plastic piping systems and had opened a second branch in Cleveland.

The next six decades were the same idea at larger scale. Thermoplastics in the 70s. A regional branch network through the 80s and 90s. Metals, instrumentation, pumps, and fabrication in the 2000s, so one supplier could cover the whole process system instead of one component of it.

Since 2020, eight specialist companies have joined the group. Each keeps its own name, its own people, and its own customers. What they share is the supply chain, the engineering bench, and the phone number.

Timeline

From one counter to ninety.

The milestones that changed what we could do for a customer.

  1. 1959Hank Harrington opens Harrington Industrial Plastics in Lynwood, California, selling plastic repair parts to wholesalers and plants.
  2. 1960Full plastic pipe and fittings distribution begins. First branch outside California opens in Cleveland.
  3. 1972Expansion into thermoplastics and corrosion-resistant systems, the foundation of what we do today.
  4. 1975First Pittsburgh-area branch opens in Braddock, Pennsylvania. Regional growth follows through the 80s and 90s.
  5. 2001First online store launches. Customers can check stock and order without calling.
  6. 2006National expansion adds branches in key industrial and ultra-pure markets.
  7. 2008Harrington University begins: formal product and application training for every branch.
  8. 2011More than 50 branches across the United States.
  9. 2015New blue-drop logo. Water, purity, and corrosive service in one mark.
  10. 201960th anniversary.
  11. 2020Aetna Plastics joins, adding plastic fabrication capacity.
  12. 2022Crist Group joins, deepening high-purity work for semiconductor and data centers.
  13. 2023PumpMan joins. Pump sales, service, and repair nationwide.
  14. 2024Fluid Gauge, Cortrol, and J2 Resources join, extending the metals side and the Gulf Coast footprint.
  15. 2026Ninety branches in 34 states. One phone number.

What we stand for

Think big. Act small. Succeed together.

Three things we hold ourselves to, in every branch and every company in the group.

  1. Technical expertiseWe are application people first. The branch rep who quotes your job can also tell you why CPVC and not PVC, and when neither will do.
  2. Specialized productsEngineered plastic and metal systems for corrosive, high-purity, harsh, and sanitary service, not a general catalog with a plastics aisle.
  3. National scale, local executionOne price book, one account team, and one set of standards. Local stock, local delivery, and a counter you can drive to.
  4. Safety before scheduleAssociate safety comes ahead of deadlines and financial results. A safe crew is the dependable one.
