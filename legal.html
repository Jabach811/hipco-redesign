California Proposition 65

Why some products carry a warning label.

A California law, not a statement about how a product performs.

WARNING: Some products sold by Harrington can expose you to chemicals, including lead and phthalates, which are known to the State of California to cause cancer, birth defects, or other reproductive harm. For more information go to www.P65Warnings.ca.gov.

What Proposition 65 is

Proposition 65, formally the Safe Drinking Water and Toxic Enforcement Act of 1986, requires businesses to warn Californians about significant exposures to chemicals the state has listed as causing cancer or reproductive harm. The list holds more than 900 chemicals and includes materials common in industrial products, such as lead in brass, PVC additives, and certain solvents.

What it means for the products we sell

Many pipe, valve, fitting, and equipment lines we distribute include materials on the list, so the manufacturer prints the warning on the product, the packaging, or the data sheet. The warning is required by California law when a listed chemical is present above a set threshold. It does not mean the product is unsafe when installed and used as intended, and it does not mean the product fails any performance or plumbing standard.

Where to find product-specific information

Safety data sheets and Proposition 65 statements come from each manufacturer. If you need one for a specific part, ask a specialist and we will get it from the source.
