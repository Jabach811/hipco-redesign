Plastic-lined steel

Dufline makes steel pipe and fittings with a plastic liner inside. You get the strength and pressure rating of steel with the chemical resistance of PTFE, PFA, or polypropylene. Made in Gonzales, Louisiana.

  • Lined pipe spools to your drawing
  • Elbows, tees, reducers, and flanges
  • Expansion joints and sight glasses
  • Field-flangeable and rotatable ends
  • Vacuum and full-vacuum ratings
  • Fast-turn replacements for outages

Liners PTFE · PFA · PVDF · PP · ETFE
