Flowline Ultrasonic Level Sensor

Harrington is a supplier of high-quality flowline ultrasonic level sensors. We carry a wide range of styles and options. The flowline ultrasonic level sensor provides a reliable level measurement of non-foaming chemical, water, wastewater, and petroleum-based liquids in ambient tank or sump applications from 1-12 meters.
