Compression Tube Fittings

Harrington is a leading supplier of high-quality compression tube fittings. Our extensive selection includes a variety of brands, styles, connection types, materials, and specifications. Compression tube fittings are used to connect two tubes or thin-walled pipes together, or to attach tubing to threaded components like valves. Compression fittings use a type of compression connection to provide a fail safe joint. Available material types include PVC, PVDF, Nitrile, Viton, Kynar, Nylon, Celcon, 302 Stainless Steel, Plastic, EPDM, and 316 Stainless Steel.
