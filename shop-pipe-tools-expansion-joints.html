Expansion Joints

Harrington is a leading supplier of high-quality expansion joints. Expansion joints are designed to hold parts together while accommodating the expansion and contraction of building materials due to temperature changes and other movements. These joints are commonly used in structures such as buildings, bridges, sidewalks, piping systems, and more.
