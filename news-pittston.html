Harrington opens new distribution center in Pittston, PA

A Northeast hub that puts more stock within a day of New York, New Jersey, and Pennsylvania plants.

We were proud to celebrate the opening of our new distribution center in Pittston Township, Pennsylvania with a ribbon cutting ceremony alongside state and local officials, business leaders, and community partners.

Located at 515 Research Drive in CenterPoint Commerce & Trade Park East, this new facility expands Harrington's presence in the Northeast and strengthens our ability to serve customers across the Northeast and Mid-Atlantic.

With more than 100,000 square feet of space, the distribution center allows us to grow our inventory, enhance our supply chain and value-added service capabilities, and provide faster, more efficient service to customers throughout the region. This expansion reflects Harrington's continued commitment to delivering industry-leading solutions and support through our nationwide network.
